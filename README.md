**SlapDesk** isn't just an application—it's a physical bridge between your workspace and your digital world. Developed by **Chaudary Global**, SlapDesk utilizes advanced vibration-sensing technology to turn your physical desk into an interactive trigger.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **🎯 Ultra-Responsive Vibration Sensing**: Our proprietary algorithm detects physical slaps and vibrations on your desk surface with adjustable sensitivity.
- **🔊 Immersion Soundscapes**: Instant auditory feedback for every physical interaction. Choose from a curated list of sounds or unlock custom responses.
- **✨ Premium UI/UX**: A sleek, modern dashboard designed for the ultimate user experience, featuring glassmorphism and real-time slap monitoring.
- **💎 Lightweight & Stable**: Built on the robust Electron framework, SlapDesk runs silently in the background with minimal system resources.
- **📦 Professional Installer**: A full, legitimate Windows installation wizard including a License Agreement (EULA) and automated shortcuts.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

### Installation
1. Download the latest **`SlapDesk Setup.exe`** from the [Releases](https://github.com/ChaudaryGlobal/slapdesk/releases) tab.
2. Run the installer wizard.
3. Accept the Terms of Service provided by **Chaudary Global**.
4. Choose your installation directory.
5. Launch SlapDesk from your Desktop or Start Menu.

### Usage
- Open the dashboard and adjust the **Sensitivity Slider** to match your desk type (Wooden, Glass, Metal).
- Choose your preferred trigger sound.
- Minimize to the system tray for a background experience.
- **Slap your desk, and let the application respond!**
